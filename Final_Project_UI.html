About Us..

MERA (often known by its social handles like @shopatmera on TikTok and Instagram) is a rapidly growing Filipino fashion and lifestyle brand that specializes in elevated everyday essentials and high-quality minimalist pieces. Founded by local entrepreneur Katty Mera Tilao-Bach, the brand has captured a massive following among young adults for its focus on modern, body-flattering silhouettes that balance current trends with timeless wearability.
